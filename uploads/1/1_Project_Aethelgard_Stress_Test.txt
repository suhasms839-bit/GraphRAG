Project Aethelgard Briefing
Internal Document | Classification: Proprietary | Date: April 2026
1. Overview
Project Aethelgard is a multi-divisional initiative within Titan Strategic (TS) aimed at
developing  the  Aether-Core,  a  sustainable  energy  regulator  designed  for  high-
density urban environments. The project represents a significant leap in power-grid
stabilization technology. 
The project is led by Dr. Elena Vance, who reports directly to the Chief Technology
Officer (CTO), Marcus Thorne. Under their guidance, the project has entered its final
integration phase. 
Strategic Note: The Aether-Core relies on proprietary modular architecture to
ensure rapid deployment and scalability across diverse geographical locations. 
2. Operational Structure
The initiative is split into three primary squads, each tasked with a critical component
of the system:
The Prism Squad: Responsible for the physical housing and thermal dissipation
of the core. They utilize advanced Crystalline-X composite materials for
superior heat resistance. Lead engineer: Sarah Chen.
The Flux Squad: Manages the energy distribution algorithms and software
stability. They developed the Overclock-Protocol to maximize energy
throughput during peak demands. Lead developer: Jameson Wu.
The Sentinel Squad: Ensures safety and system security. They implement 
Black-Box Logic to prevent system breaches and unauthorized access.

3. Key Dependencies and Interconnects
The success of Project Aethelgard is underpinned by several historical and corporate
relationships:
Academic Ties: Sarah Chen and Jameson Wu are both alumni of the Aurelius
Institute, which provided the initial patent for the Aether-Core technology.
Security Rationale: Dr. Elena Vance previously worked at Sentinel Squad’s
primary competitor, Aegis Systems. This prior experience is the primary reason
she insists on the strict implementation of Black-Box Logic for internal security.
Supply Chain: The Crystalline-X material is sourced exclusively from Northern
Extractives, a specialized subsidiary of Titan Strategic.
